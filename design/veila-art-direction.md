## Photography prompt set

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool; original PNGs remain in the generation library. Project JPEGs live in `design/assets/veila-products/`.

Shared prompt: square photorealistic premium skincare editorial photograph for VEILA SKIN. Complete product with generous margins, frosted glass or matte tube, brushed silver/chrome cap, dark serif VEILA with small SKIN below. Warm ivory studio, pale limestone plinth, soft oblique morning light from the upper left, gentle shadows, tactile stone, botanical props near the lower corners. No percentages, efficacy claims, watermark, people, floating ingredients or captions. Brand style reference: the approved Veila hero bottle. Ingredient props illustrate proposed concepts, not a verified formula.

- cleanser: tall ivory-sage pump, oat stems, aloe slice, clear glycerin-inspired drop.
