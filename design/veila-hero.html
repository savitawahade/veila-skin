A few thoughtful details

Good to know.

How do I explore the collection?

Browse by category, search a product or proposed ingredient, and sort by price. Open any product to see its size, texture direction and place in the Veila collection.

Are these products available to buy?

This is the first Veila Skin concept storefront. The bag lets you try the shopping experience, but checkout and payments are not connected. Prices and packaging are illustrative.

Where can I find the ingredients?

Every product has visible ingredient notes, an interactive ingredient story and detailed product information. These are proposed directions, not finalized formulas. Full ingredient lists, suitability and application directions will be added before products launch.

What about delivery and returns?

Delivery areas, timelines and returns will be confirmed when Veila is ready to accept orders. No shipping or return promise is made in this preview.
