exact wide framing, sage bottle, ivory bulb, brushed silver collar, thick

remain inside the upper empty third. Bottle stays fixed at x=70% of frame.
Aloe, oats, pedestal and lighting stay fixed.

Three key poses: closed bottle at 0s; dropper lifted straight above the same
neck at 5s; exactly the same closed bottle at 10s.
0–2s: closed still beauty shot.
2–4s: collar releases and dropper rises vertically without tilt, rotation or
sideways drift. Straight glass pipette remains precisely coaxial with neck.

7–9s: lower the same dropper vertically and seat closed.
9–10s: still closing hold.

Critical constraints: pipette never points away from neck; no droplet beside
the bottle; no cut-off cap or bulb; no extra cap; label and bottle shape never

no large floating ingredients or captions. Silent. Smooth restrained realistic
